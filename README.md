# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** August 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how **ML-Notifier** ("the Bot", "we", "us") processes information when operating in Discord servers in which it has been authorized for use.

The Bot is a privately developed, multipurpose Discord application primarily intended for moderation, administration, anti-spam, security, and community-management purposes.

## **1. Information the Bot may process**

Depending on the features enabled in a Discord server, the Bot may access or process information made available through Discord, including:

- Discord user IDs;
- usernames, display names, and nicknames;
- Discord server and channel IDs;
- member roles and permissions;
- member join and leave information;
- moderation actions such as warnings, timeouts, mutes, kicks, bans, and related administrative actions;
- information associated with moderation or support tickets;
- messages, message metadata, attachments, embeds, links, and other message-related information where required for moderation or Bot functionality;
- records of commands or actions performed through the Bot;
- information necessary to detect spam, abuse, repeated messages, prohibited content, or other violations of server rules.

The exact information processed depends on the features being used.

## **2. Message Content**

The Bot may inspect message content where necessary to provide moderation, anti-spam, administrative, or other configured functionality.

For example, recent message content may be temporarily retained in application memory to identify repeated-message spam.

The Bot is not intended to operate as a general-purpose archive of users' private conversations.

Message content is not sold or used for advertising, and it is not used to train artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models.

Where message content or related evidence forms part of a moderation or security incident, relevant information may be associated with a moderation record where necessary for investigating or documenting that incident, subject to applicable Discord requirements and law.

## **3. Why Information Is Processed**

Information may be processed for purposes including:

- operating Bot functionality;
- enforcing server rules;
- detecting and preventing spam or abuse;
- investigating security or moderation incidents;
- maintaining moderation and administrative records;
- preventing repeated abuse;
- managing server membership and roles;
- handling support or moderation tickets;
- responding to user or moderator commands;
- maintaining the security and integrity of the Discord community and the Bot;
- troubleshooting errors and maintaining the Bot.

Information obtained through Discord is not used for unrelated advertising, marketing, user profiling, or sale to data brokers.

## **4. Moderation and Security Records**

Moderation and security records may be retained for extended periods where they remain necessary for server moderation, enforcement of sanctions, abuse prevention, security, investigation of incidents, or resolution of moderation disputes.

For example, information concerning a serious or permanent moderation action may remain relevant for as long as the associated sanction remains in effect.

Retention of information is nevertheless subject to applicable law and Discord's Developer Terms and policies. Information will not be retained solely for unrelated purposes after it is no longer necessary for operation of the Bot or permitted moderation and security purposes.

## **5. Support and Ticket Information**

Information submitted through support or moderation tickets may be processed and retained for the purposes of resolving the ticket, maintaining administrative records, preventing abuse, enforcing server rules, and resolving future disputes relating to the matter.

Users should avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive personal information through tickets or Bot commands.

## **6. Data Sharing**

Information processed by the Bot is not sold.

Discord API Data is not provided to advertisers or data brokers.

Information may only be disclosed where necessary:

- to authorized server administrators or moderators for legitimate moderation and administration purposes;
- to service providers necessary for operation of the Bot, where permitted;
- where required by applicable law;
- where required by Discord;
- where disclosure is necessary to investigate or respond to security incidents, subject to applicable requirements.

## **7. Data Storage and Security**

Information stored by the Bot outside Discord is hosted on secured cloud infrastructure. Persistent storage containing Discord API Data is protected using encryption at rest provided by the hosting infrastructure.

Access to the Bot, its databases, and stored information is limited to the Bot administrator and to services technically necessary for operation of the Bot.

Technical and administrative safeguards are used at both the hosting-infrastructure and application level to reduce the risk of unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss. The hosting provider maintains security controls for its underlying infrastructure, while the Bot administrator is responsible for securing the operating system, Bot software, databases, credentials, and application configuration.

Server, database, and application credentials are kept private and are not intentionally disclosed to unauthorized third parties.

Where information is transferred over a network, secure communication methods are used where supported and appropriate.

Backups or other persistent copies containing Discord API Data are handled using storage and infrastructure intended to provide equivalent protection, including encryption at rest where applicable.

## **8. Data Retention**

Different types of information may have different retention periods depending on their purpose.

Temporary information, such as data used solely for short-term spam detection, may be removed automatically when it is no longer required.

Administrative, moderation, security, ban, and abuse-prevention records may be retained for substantially longer where they continue to be necessary for those purposes.

Retention is subject to Discord's Developer Terms, applicable law, and any obligations requiring information to be deleted or retained.

Moderation decisions and data-retention obligations are separate matters.
